## The Spark: Diploma Days in Visakhapatnam

When I was doing my diploma in EEE branch in Visakhapatnam, I still remember my teachers who taught me the technology for the first time. Electrical subjects with enthusiasm — they showed me the taste of technology. They played with our curiosity by asking questions and later clearing them.

This early exposure taught me something fundamental: **technology isn't just about memorizing formulas; it's about feeling how things work.**

## The Bridge: JNTU Kakinada

During my B.Tech at JNTU Kakinada, my analytical ability and enthusiasm further increased towards computerization of all Electrical networks. My final year project — **Computer Methods in Power System Analysis** — was where I first saw the intersection of traditional electrical engineering and computational thinking.

The realization hit me: the same principles governing power flow in electrical networks could be modeled, simulated, and optimized through code.

## The Pivot: Andhra University

Joining Andhra University for my part-time M.Tech in Computer Science Engineering felt like a second innings of my college days. A prestigious university with a huge campus and big libraries. Here, as an Electrical Engineer by degree and Communication Engineer by profession, I entered the world of Computer Science Engineering as my passion forced me.

The CCNA knowledge and departmental trainings from BSNL made this transition possible.

## The Passion: Cyber Security & Ethical Hacking

Then my passion took a new turn. While attending seminars and workshops at the university, I took on the challenge of the present-day digital atmosphere of the Internet to test my luck in Cyber Security.

Two people indirectly inspired this juncture:

1. **Ankit Fadia** — An ethical hacker from Mumbai. I completed the AFCEH (Ankit Fadia Certified Ethical Hacker) online certificate course.
2. **Mahesh Rakheja** — A techie from Nagpur. Beyond cyber security, I learned the way of having fun in delivering lectures, and how to make an audience come alive.

## My Philosophy

> **Playing with technology gives me peace. Sharing technology gives me happiness.**

I believe in **feeling the technology rather than just learning**. I am passionate about sharing and letting people enjoy the technology. Happily doing the task of encouraging people towards tasting the sweetness in technology.

With God's grace, support of my officers, love of my family, and friends — I continue this journey.
